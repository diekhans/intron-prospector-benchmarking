#+TITLE: Why junctions_from_sam reports WTC11 junctions the intron-prospector calls do not
#+DATE: 2026-11-16

* Conclusion

Every junction counted as flair-only under filt_similar comes from reads that
align in more than one place.  Two separate causes, neither a defect in either
tool:

- 7,968 of 14,763 are reported by all three tools.  filt_similar removes them
  from the STAR and intron-prospector sides for having no uniquely mapped read
  or an anchor below 12, and bin/sj-compare cannot apply either threshold to
  the flair side, so they survive there alone.
- 6,794 of 14,763 are absent from the calls at any threshold.  They are
  supported only by secondary alignments, SAM flag 0x100, which
  intron-prospector excludes by default and junctions_from_sam never tests for.
  20 of 20 sampled junctions are of this kind.
- 1 of 14,763 is reported by junctions_from_sam alone, also from two secondary
  alignments.

The secondary run, --include=qcfail,secondary, confirms it: the calls then hold
all 292,346 junctions junctions_from_sam reports, and flair-only under raw is 0.

flair's total is the smaller one, 292,346 junctions against 356,191, because
junctions_from_sam skips every alignment whose CIGAR holds an insertion,
deletion, soft clip or hard clip.  Flair-only is a statement about which 14,763
junctions it has, not about how many.

* Population

output/WTC11.qcfail.filt_similar.summary.tsv, main chromosomes only, the qcfail
run, so the QC fail alignments of doc/wtc11-star-only.org are already counted.

| side              | junctions | only this side |
|-------------------+-----------+----------------|
| STAR              |   356,424 |             98 |
| intron-prospector |   356,191 |             23 |
| junctions_from_sam |  292,346 |         14,763 |

junctions_from_sam has 14,935 junctions the calls do not, of which 14,763 are
in neither of the other two sets.

* Method

- Took the 14,763 rows with found = flair from
  output/WTC11.qcfail.filt_similar.compare.tsv.gz.
- Looked each up in output/WTC11.qcfail.raw.compare.tsv.gz, the same two sets
  with no threshold applied to either.
- For the ones absent from the calls there as well, drew 20 with
  random.Random(20261116).sample, fetched every alignment covering the last
  exon base from the BAM, parsed all N operations out of the CIGAR, kept those
  whose N matches the junction exactly, and classified each by SAM flag.
- Indexed the BAM into data/WTC11.illumina.bam.bai, since its own directory is
  not writable.

* Result

Where the 14,763 are in the raw comparison:

| found, no filter      |     n | cause                                     |
|-----------------------+-------+-------------------------------------------|
| star+prospector+flair | 7,968 | removed from two sides by filt_similar    |
| star+flair            | 6,794 | supported only by secondary alignments    |
| flair                 |     1 | supported only by secondary alignments    |

** The 7,968 that filt_similar removes

Every one fails a threshold on the intron-prospector side, none is unexplained:

| reason                                        |     n |
|-----------------------------------------------+-------|
| uniq_mapped_count 0, anchor at least 12       | 7,136 |
| anchor below 12, uniq_mapped_count at least 1 |    19 |
| both                                          |   813 |

The STAR side fails the same thresholds, 7,948 with a unique count of 0 and 101
with an overhang below 12, so the junction is dropped from two sides of three.
junctions_from_sam reports neither an anchor nor a unique count, so
bin/sj-compare has nothing to apply its thresholds to and leaves the flair side
whole.  The junction is then the only member of its side.

** The 6,794 that the calls do not contain

| property           | value                                      |
|--------------------+--------------------------------------------|
| STAR unique count  | 0 for all 6,794                            |
| STAR multi count   | 159,612 alignments in all                  |
| annotated in STAR  | 6,531 of 6,794                             |
| motif              | 6,730 GT/AG, 47 GC/AG, 17 non-canonical    |
| flair count 1 / 2  | 238 / 1,361                                |
| flair count 3-9    | 2,853                                      |
| flair count 10-99  | 2,056                                      |
| flair count 100+   | 286                                        |

A unique count of 0 on the STAR side for all of them is the whole statement:
these junctions are crossed only by reads that align elsewhere as well.  Most
are annotated and canonical, so they are real junctions of real genes, reported
from the wrong locus or from one of several equally good ones.

Sample of 20, the same classification as doc/wtc11-star-only.org:

|---------------------------------------------------+----|
| supported only by secondary alignments            | 20 |
| supported only by QC fail alignments              |  0 |
| supported by an alignment intron-prospector reads |  0 |
| no alignment has the N operation                  |  0 |

Supporting alignment counts run from 1 to 43 in the sample, all secondary.

* Root cause

Which SAM flag bits each tool acts on, the same table as doc/wtc11-star-only.org
read along the 0x100 row:

|   bit | name      | SJ.out.tab          | intron-prospector                | junctions_from_sam |
|-------+-----------+---------------------+----------------------------------+--------------------|
| 0x100 | SECONDARY | counted in column 8 | excluded unless --include=secondary | counted         |
| 0x200 | QCFAIL    | counted             | excluded unless --include=qcfail  | counted            |

- intron-prospector: ALIGN_CATS_EXCLUDED_DEFAULT in src/junctions_extractor.hh
  holds BAM_FQCFAIL, BAM_FDUP and BAM_FSECONDARY.  A multi-mapping read is
  counted, in multi_mapped_count, but only through its primary alignment
  record.  For these junctions the primary alignment is at another locus, so
  nothing records them.
- junctions_from_sam: src/flair/junctions_from_sam.py reads the FLAG field into
  a variable and never tests it.  It skips an unmapped record, and a CIGAR with
  I, D, S or H, and nothing else.
- SJ.out.tab: counts these alignments in column 8, multi-mapping reads, which
  is why column 7 is 0 and the junction is still in the tab.

* Evidence

- Region reproduction.  chr12:14705000-14711000 extracted to its own BAM and
  run twice with the thresholds bin/run-prospector uses:

  : samtools view -X -b -o region.bam BAM BAI chr12:14705000-14711000
  : intron-prospector --min-anchor-length=1 --min-confidence-score=0.0 \
  :     --sj-filter=all --intron-calls=OUT region.bam

  The default run reports nothing at 14705629.  Adding --include=secondary, and
  nothing else, reports it:

  : chr12  14705629  14710398  .  uniq 0  multi 1003  left 99  right 99  conf 4.55

  That is the minimum controlling change.

- Supporting alignments.  1,753 alignments cross chr12:14705629-14710398, every
  one secondary, NH 2 to 20, from 1,058 distinct reads: 363 reads with one
  alignment and 695 with two, their mates.  86 alignments also have the QC fail
  flag, which the region run above did not include, which is why it reports
  1,003 reads rather than 1,058.  With --include=qcfail,secondary it reports
  1,058, the multi-mapping count SJ.out.tab has for this junction.
  Two more junctions checked the same way: chr12:7819583-7925843, 2,522
  alignments, and chr16:14834016-14838406, 1,191 alignments, all secondary.

- The single flair-only junction in the raw comparison,
  chr12:120321018-120502136, has two alignments, both flag 419, which is 0x100
  plus proper pair, second in pair and mate reverse, both three-exon CIGARs with
  a 13M or 14M anchor.

* Consequences

- The remaining read-set difference against junctions_from_sam is secondary
  alignments.  The qcfail run closed the QC fail one.
- A junction crossed only by secondary alignments is invisible to
  intron-prospector by design: its reads have a better alignment somewhere else.
  Whether to count it is a question about the dataset, not about the tool.
- The benchmark now has a secondary run, --include=qcfail,secondary, which makes
  the read sets equal: the BAMs have no duplicate flags, and both tools read
  supplementary alignments.

* What --include=qcfail,secondary does to the comparison

output/WTC11.<run>.<filter>.summary.tsv, main chromosomes only.

| run       | filter       |    STAR | intron-prospector | in flair, not in the calls | only flair |
|-----------+--------------+---------+-------------------+----------------------------+------------|
| qcfail    | raw          | 383,247 |           370,242 |                      6,795 |          1 |
| secondary | raw          | 383,247 |           383,256 |                          0 |          0 |
| qcfail    | filt_similar | 356,424 |           356,191 |                     14,935 |     14,763 |
| secondary | filt_similar | 356,424 |           356,462 |                     14,761 |     14,759 |
| qcfail    | filt_flair   | 383,247 |           271,976 |                     36,793 |          1 |
| secondary | filt_flair   | 383,247 |           292,280 |                     19,709 |          1 |

- Under raw the calls become a superset of the flair set: 0 of 292,346 missing,
  which is the prediction of this investigation run against the whole BAM
  rather than a sample.
- The junctions STAR reports and the calls do not also fall from 13,009 to 3
  under raw, so secondary alignments account for nearly all of what the qcfail
  run left of doc/wtc11-star-only.org.  intron-prospector then reports 11
  junctions SJ.out.tab does not.
- Under filt_similar the flair-only count stays at 14,759.  These are the
  junctions with no uniquely mapped read; a secondary alignment is counted as
  multi-mapping, so reading it does not move a junction over that threshold.
  The number falls by only 4, where a secondary alignment supplies an anchor of
  12 or more.
- Under filt_flair 19,709 junctions junctions_from_sam reports still fail the
  confidence threshold on the calls side, down from 36,793.  Both tools compute
  the same statistic over different alignment sets: junctions_from_sam skips a
  CIGAR with an insertion, deletion or clip and counts each alignment, while
  intron-prospector counts each read, so a pair whose mates both cross a
  junction gives one offset on the calls side and two on the flair side.  That
  is a different cause from this one.
